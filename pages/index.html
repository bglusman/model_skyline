Interpretation boundary

Static aliases do not enforce freshness. Check each manifest and source watermark before use. Reconstructed token costs exclude cache behavior, tool and infrastructure cost, discounts, availability, and invoice reconciliation. The publications contain no live routing selection and are not safe to consume as an automatic model default. Their hashes detect mutation but do not authenticate the publisher.
