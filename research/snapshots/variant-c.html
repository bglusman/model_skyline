Variant C — the budget explorable

Design thesis: don't show the reader a frontier — let them push on it. Pick a workload, a budget, and a taste for smarts, and the page computes your personal pareto pick from the same ranked[] rows, live, showing what you'd have to give up to buy the next intelligence step.

All selection runs client-side over the printed frontier data. † = UNVERIFIED modeled cap; sub offerings are priced per-turn from published tables (capped purchases modeled at 8×). No backend, no inference — the frontier is the interface.
