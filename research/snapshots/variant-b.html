Variant B — the annotated staircase

Design thesis: a Pareto frontier is a staircase, not a scatterplot — draw the dominant steps in plain SVG, label every step directly with the offering and its exact prices, drop a ghost dot for every dominated model with a thin arrow to the step it fails to reach, and place one editorial callout per chart naming the trade the data actually argues for.

Solid dots + stepped line = frontier · gold dot = dominated within 10% of the step it shadows · gray dots = dominated (arrow points at the frontier step that dominates them) · † = UNVERIFIED modeled cap. Hover a point to see exact values.
