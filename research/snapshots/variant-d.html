Variant D — model dossiers

Design thesis: the frontier asks “which models win?” — but a buyer's real question is “who is this model for?” So: one dossier card per model, built bottom-up from its actual frontiers, with exact numbers, a per-frontier cost-slash-line (dot on a 10×-spaced ruler), its nearest rival at every frontier, and a computed verdict sentence.

Each card is computed from ranked[] — no hand-written copy. † = UNVERIFIED modeled cap. ★ on frontier · ≈ within 10% of frontier · · dominated.
